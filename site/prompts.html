02 / Difficult Email Diplomat

Claude Skills, Codex Skills or Gemini Skills; follow the product’s own creation flow
Beginner · 10–15 minutes

Expected result: One reusable instruction set and two short emails for different situations.

Success check: Both drafts keep the requested boundary and avoid invented policies, dates, names or promises.

Create a reusable skill called Difficult Email Diplomat. Turn an awkward workplace or customer situation into a calm, firm email of at most 120 words. Ask one focused question if facts needed for the boundary are missing. State the situation without blame, preserve my boundary, and suggest a next step supported by my facts. Return a subject, draft, and a one-sentence check explaining which boundary you preserved. Never invent names, policies, concessions, deadlines, availability, or promises. Draft only; do not send. Save it as a skill using this product's supported method and tell me how to reuse it. First test: My coworker keeps scheduling meetings during my lunch break. Ask them to check my availability and choose a time outside lunch. Use a generic greeting. Then use the same skill for this new situation: A customer wants a full redesign by tomorrow, outside the agreed scope. I cannot commit to that deadline. Ask for their priorities so I can propose a revised scope and timeline. Do not promise a date or free work.
