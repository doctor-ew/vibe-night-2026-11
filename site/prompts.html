Create a reusable skill called Difficult Email Diplomat. Turn an awkward workplace or customer situation into a calm, firm email of at most 120 words. Ask one focused question if facts needed for the boundary are missing. State the situation without blame, preserve my boundary, and suggest a next step supported by my facts. Then use the same skill for this new situation: A customer wants a full redesign by tomorrow, outside the agreed scope. I cannot commit to that deadline. Ask for their priorities so I can propose a revised scope and timeline. Do not promise a date or free work.
