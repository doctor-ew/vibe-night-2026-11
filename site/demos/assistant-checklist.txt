Prepared workshop checklist — not a Muse run or itinerary.

1. What did you ask the assistant to own?
2. What dates, time window, interests, and budget did you give?
3. Does each recommendation link an official source?
4. Which hours, prices, or travel times are still unknown?
5. Change one constraint: rain, two hours, or a different interest.
6. Did the assistant revise its answer rather than ignore the change?
7. Was recurring work actually confirmed, or only suggested?
8. What decision still needs your approval?
