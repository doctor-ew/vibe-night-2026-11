Subject: Meeting times around lunch

Hi,

I’ve noticed several meetings have landed during my lunch break. I need to keep that time free. Could you check my availability and choose a time outside lunch for future meetings?

Thanks for helping me protect that break.

Check: The draft clearly protects lunch and asks for a different meeting time without inventing a policy or blaming the recipient.
