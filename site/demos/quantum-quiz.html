Can a qubit reveal every answer?

A bit has a value of 0 or 1. A qubit can be prepared in a superposition. A standard 0/1 measurement gives one result, not a list of every possibility.

Think of overlapping waves that can reinforce or cancel. The wave comparison helps explain interference; it is not a literal picture of a qubit.
